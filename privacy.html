Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains how Jasper Solar Solutions (GSTIN 32CZHPA7698N1ZR), Nedumangad, Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala, India (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you enquire about, buy or use our rooftop solar products and services — including when you contact us through our website, Facebook or Instagram lead forms, WhatsApp, phone or in person. It also covers our customer management system (“Solarize CRM”) used by our staff.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact details — name, mobile number, email address, city and address you share with us.
  • Enquiry details — answers you give in our forms (for example system size, electricity bill, roof type, preferred visit date).
  • Project and installation details — KSEB consumer number, sanctioned load, site photos, documents you provide for KSEB, MNRE / PM Surya Ghar subsidy and loan applications, payment receipts, invoices and warranty records.
  • Messages — WhatsApp messages, photos and documents you send to our business number, and our replies, together with delivery and read status.
  • Advertising lead data — when you submit a Facebook or Instagram lead form for one of our ads, Meta shares the information you entered in that form with us, along with the campaign and ad it came from.

We do not collect payment card details through the CRM, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry, call or message you, arrange site visits and send quotations and product datasheets you request.
  • To design, supply, install and commission your solar system and provide maintenance (AMC) and service.
  • To complete KSEB, DISCOM, MNRE / PM Surya Ghar subsidy and loan formalities on your behalf.
  • To issue invoices, record payments, deliver warranty cards and meet our legal and tax obligations.
  • To send service updates, reminders and, where permitted, information about our offers. You can ask us to stop promotional messages at any time.

3. WhatsApp

We use the WhatsApp Business Platform (provided by Meta Platforms, Inc.) to communicate with customers. When you message our business number, your WhatsApp profile name, number and messages are received by our CRM so our team can reply and keep a record linked to your enquiry or project. WhatsApp's own handling of your data is governed by the WhatsApp Privacy Policy. We only send you messages about your enquiry or project, or messages you have agreed to receive. Reply STOP at any time to opt out of non-essential messages.

4. Facebook and Instagram lead forms

If you submit a lead form on one of our Facebook or Instagram ads, the details you enter are shared with us by Meta and imported into our CRM so our team can contact you about solar for your home or business. Meta's handling of your data is governed by the Meta Privacy Policy.

5. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only where needed to provide our services:

  • Kerala State Electricity Board (KSEB) / DISCOM, MNRE and the PM Surya Ghar portal, for connection, net-metering and subsidy applications.
  • Banks or finance partners, only if you apply for a loan through us.
  • Equipment manufacturers and distributors, for warranty registration and claims.
  • Service providers that host or operate our systems (for example our web hosting provider and Meta for WhatsApp and lead forms), under confidentiality obligations.
  • Government or legal authorities, where required by law.

6. How long we keep information

Enquiry details are kept while we are in contact with you and for up to 3 years afterwards. Customer, project, warranty, invoice and subsidy records are kept for as long as needed for your system's warranty and service life and for the periods required by tax and electricity regulations. Messages are kept as part of your customer record for the same period.

7. How we protect information

Our CRM is only accessible to authorised staff with individual logins and role-based permissions. Data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections, stored on access-restricted servers, documents are not publicly accessible, and actions are recorded in an audit log. Access tokens for Meta and WhatsApp are stored on our server and never shown in the browser.

8. Your rights and data deletion

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop contacting you. To make a request:

Send a WhatsApp message to our business number saying “Delete my data”, or contact us by phone or in person at our office in Nedumangad, Thiruvananthapuram. Please include the name and mobile number you used with us.

We will confirm your request within 7 days and complete it within 30 days. We may keep records that we are legally required to retain (for example tax invoices and subsidy records), and will tell you if that applies.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows when it was last changed.
